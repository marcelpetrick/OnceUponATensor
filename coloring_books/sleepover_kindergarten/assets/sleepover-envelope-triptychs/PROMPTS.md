# Sleepover envelope triptychs

These ten PNG files were generated with the built-in image generation tool on
2026-07-23. Every final canvas has an exact 3:1 pixel ratio and contains three
equal square scenes separated by narrow white gutters.

## Shared prompt

Use case: illustration-story

Asset type: printable horizontal three-panel cover strip for the front of a
letter envelope

Create one complete high-resolution artwork on an exact 3:1 horizontal canvas.
It contains exactly three equal square panels side by side, each one third of
the width, separated by narrow clean white gutters/cut lines. No title, words,
logo, watermark, decorative border, or extra panel.

Lock the same protagonist across all three panels: one cheerful, athletic,
slim-but-healthy boy aged five to six, with the same face and proportions,
shoulder-length blond hair in a visible small sporty bun, and a few loose
strands. He is an original child character, not a specific real person. He
wears an original, unbranded red Norway-inspired football jersey or light
sports jacket with only narrow blue-and-white sleeve/shoulder piping, dark blue
shorts, children's sandals, and green/blue accents. His soft black frog bucket
hat has two raised frog eyes and one tiny friendly frog face. The jersey front
stays solid red: no Scandinavian cross, flag, shield, badge, sponsor, name,
number, emblem, trademark, or copied logo. Never duplicate the protagonist
inside a panel. Keep full-body silhouettes readable and leave generous white
space.

Left panel: the boy dominates, smiling and dynamically dribbling one normal
football outdoors. Three or four kindergarten friends run and laugh as smaller
secondary figures. He wears the frog hat; his bun and hair remain readable.
Show only pale green grass, one softly suggested goal, and at most one faint
tree.

Middle panel: a cozy Pfadfinder/scout overnight gathering with only four or
five children total in pale blue and green sleeping bags arranged in a loose
circle. The boy sits upright, attentive and smiling. His head is bare so his
blond bun is visible, and his single frog hat lies directly beside his
blue-green sleeping bag. A tall, slim, friendly adult leader with fair skin and
an unmistakably shoulder-length blond bob reads to the children. Her small
booklet is angled toward her so the viewer sees only its completely blank pale
back cover: no visible pages, writing lines, symbols, pseudo-letters, or
markings. Use a minimal cream/pale-blue wash with perhaps one tiny lantern,
faint moon, or subtle stars.

Right panel: the boy dominates, walking forward joyfully on his first day of
school with a large traditional German Schultüte. The cone is pale green and
blue with simple stars, one small football motif, and a soft tissue-paper top.
He wears the same clothes, sandals, backpack, bun, and frog hat. One welcoming
adult woman has an original, generic Alpine/Nordic outdoorsy appearance: tall,
feminine, warm, fair-skinned, with long flowing or braided hair and understated
alpine-inspired clothing. She is not a specific person. At most one or two
faint schoolchildren appear near a pale doorway with minimal entrance lines.

Use a pale muted palette of powder blue, pale green, warm cream, light beige,
and faded red. Keep the contrast low. Heads, hands, feet, football, sleeping
bags, hats, and Schultüte stay fully inside their panels with breathing room.
Avoid photorealism, saturated color, dark backgrounds, heavy shadows, dense
scenery, clutter, extra objects, malformed anatomy, extra limbs or digits,
distorted faces or hands, and any readable or pseudo-text.

   expressive hatching, and very dilute pastel watercolor washes.
